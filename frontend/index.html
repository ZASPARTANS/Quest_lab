        How to read the blocks

        Each block is 6 hours long and starts at the time shown. The word and the drops are the rain level of the wetter of the two models; "models differ" means they are in different levels. A tick means the block fits what you want to do. Faded blocks are over or outside your hours. Press a block to see it on the map.

        What this page does

        Shows when rain is expected over the next 10 days and picks the best times for what you want to do.

        How to use it

        1. Where. Use your location, search for a town, or click the map.
        2. What. Pick an activity, such as "Drying laundry" or "Commute".
        3. Best times. See the blocks that stay under its rain limit; tap one to see it on the map.

        Which model?

        • IFS: ECMWF's physics-based forecast.
        • AIFS: ECMWF's machine-learning forecast.
        • Both models (agreement): bright blue means both say rain, dim blue means only one does. Start here.

        The plan uses both and takes the wetter one. "Models differ" means less certain.
