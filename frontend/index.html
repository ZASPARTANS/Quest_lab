Saved places

    Your location and saved places stay in this browser. Place search by Open-Meteo.com. You can also click the map.

    What is sent to whom?

    Searching sends the text you type, and your IP address, to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). Using your location sends your position and IP address to BigDataCloud (bigdatacloud.com) to get the place name. Nobody else gets anything.

        Day by day

        Each block is 6 hours long and starts at the time shown. The word and the drops are the rain level of the wetter of the two models; "models differ" means they are in different levels. A tick means the block fits what you want to do. Faded blocks are over or outside your hours. Press a block to see it on the map.
